## Design and interfaces

- Schema v3 adds beliefs, durable single-sample pending statistics, prospective
  transition queue, and processed markers. Existing rows are never backfilled.
- `Memory.record_transition_experience(world_model_eligible=False)` atomically
  queues only explicitly eligible new transitions. Autonomous B4 cycles opt in.
- `WorldModel.recover()` processes at most 100 queued IDs in ascending order.
  `observe_transition(id)` reads the persisted source, with queue deletion,
  belief/pending changes, telemetry and processed marker in one IMMEDIATE transaction.
  Refuse out-of-order processing so contradiction semantics survive crash/retry.
- Four signals, global plus four action scopes; at most 20 combined pending/belief
  rows. Two valid samples promote a pending statistic into a belief. Numeric
  incremental means use an overflow-safe weighted form for opposite extremes.
- Contradictions use pre-update n>=4, numeric epsilons .05/.02/.01, Boolean
  majority thresholds .75/.25. Confidence = min(.95,n/20)*(1-c/n).
- Dashboard reads only list/detail with bounded keyset pagination/filtering.
  No frontend, behavior feedback, predictor/evaluator edits, sensors or network.
