1. Snapshot/budget cleanup errors: prove bounded acquisition, fatal Q failures, auxiliary history fallback and connection cleanup in Task 1.

5. Observer trust and regression scope: no rescoring/writes, bounded corrupt-row pagination, B7 compatibility and unchanged historical formula evidence in Tasks 4–5.
